Sable is a desktop browser for the kind of person who keeps thirty tabs open and treats them as one document. The URL bar is the assistant. Tabs become citations with a drag. Splits work like a tiling editor. Local Qwen runs offline; bring your own key for frontier quality. Familiar at 10am Tuesday — never asks for attention it hasn't earned.

Mixtral 8×7B

a sparse mixture of experts
jiang · sablayrolles · roux · mensch · de las casas et al.

Each layer is composed of 8 feedforward blocks (i.e. experts). For every token, at each layer, a router network selects two experts to process the current state and combine their outputs.

Each token has access to 47B parameters but only uses 13B active parameters during inference. Trained with a 32k context window — outperforms Llama 2 70B and GPT-3.5 across all evaluated benchmarks.
