## Rules for the tweet

- **Always** attach a visual: a short video > hero image > nothing.
- **Always** link to `your-org.github.io/sable` (the launch site), not to GitHub directly. The launch site converts; the repo doesn't.
- **Pin it for 7 days.** New visitors land on it.
- **Don't @-mention anyone in the launch tweet itself** — looks needy. Quote-tweet supporters separately.
